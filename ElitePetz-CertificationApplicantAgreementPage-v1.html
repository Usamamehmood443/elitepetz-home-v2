Certification Applicant Agreement

This Certification Applicant Agreement ("Agreement") governs submission of an application for certification to the ElitePetz Certification Authority ("ElitePetz" or "the Authority").

Terms of the Agreement

Binding Applicant Terms
By submitting an application for certification, the Applicant affirms and agrees as follows.
SEC. 01

Nature of the Authority

  • 1.1ElitePetz is a private certification body operating within the companion animal breeding sector.
  • 1.2Certification issued by ElitePetz constitutes recognition of compliance with ElitePetz's published standards only.
  • 1.3Certification does not constitute government licensure, regulatory approval, endorsement of commercial activity, or guarantee of outcomes.
SEC. 02

Representations and Warranties

The Applicant represents and warrants that:

  • 2.1All information submitted in connection with the application is true, accurate, and complete.
  • 2.2The Applicant possesses full authority to apply on behalf of the named entity.
  • 2.3No material fact has been omitted that would reasonably affect the evaluation of the application.

The Authority may deny, suspend, or revoke certification if any representation is found to be false, misleading, or materially incomplete.

SEC. 03

Compliance with Governing Documents

The Applicant agrees to comply with all governing documents of the Authority, including:

  • Certification Standards
  • Code of Ethics
  • Terms of Use
  • Privacy Policy
  • Governance & Enforcement Framework
  • Badge Usage Policy

These documents collectively govern eligibility, certification status, and enforcement actions.

The Applicant agrees to comply with the Badge Usage Policy upon certification.

SEC. 04

Certification Determinations

  • 4.1Certification approval is not guaranteed.
  • 4.2The Authority retains sole discretion in certification determinations, subject to its published governance framework.
  • 4.3Certification status may be denied, suspended, revoked, or reinstated in accordance with established procedures.
  • 4.4All status determinations are recorded in a structured audit record maintained by the Authority.
SEC. 05

Public Registry Disclosure

If certification is granted, the Applicant acknowledges and consents to publication of certification-related information in the public registry, including:

  • Entity name
  • Certification identifier
  • Certification status
  • Activation date
  • Status history

Such publication forms part of the Authority's transparency framework.

SEC. 06

Fees

Where applicable, certification fees:

  • 6.1Are payable as established by the Authority.
  • 6.2Do not guarantee approval or continued certification.
SEC. 07

Limitation of Role

The Applicant acknowledges that the Authority:

  • ✕Does not participate in commercial transactions
  • ✕Does not act as broker, agent, or representative
  • ✕Does not resolve private disputes
  • ✕Does not guarantee animal health, quality, or contractual performance
SEC. 08

Record Integrity

The Applicant acknowledges that certification status actions are recorded as part of a structured audit log designed to preserve institutional integrity.

Such records may be retained in accordance with the Authority's data governance policies.

SEC. 09

Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Minnesota, United States.

Acknowledgment

By submitting a certification application, the Applicant acknowledges that it has read, understood, and agreed to be bound by this Agreement.
