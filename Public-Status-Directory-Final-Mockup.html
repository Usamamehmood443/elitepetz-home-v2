Understanding certification status

Only "ElitePetz Certified" indicates a certification currently in active standing.

Status key
ElitePetz Certified

Certification is active and currently recognized by the Authority.

Suspended

Certification is temporarily not in active standing while under review or a defined process.

Revoked

Certification has been formally withdrawn and is no longer recognized as active.

Expired

The certification term ended without completed renewal; the historical record remains public.
