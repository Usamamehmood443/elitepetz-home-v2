SEC. 02

Nature of the Authority

ElitePetz Certification Authority ("ElitePetz," "the Authority," "we," "us") is a private certification body operating within the companion animal breeding sector.

ElitePetz
  • ✕Is not a government agency
  • ✕Is not a licensing authority
  • ✕Is not a regulatory body
  • ✕Does not sell animals
  • ✕Does not act as a broker
  • ✕Does not represent buyers or sellers

ElitePetz certification reflects conformity with the Authority's published certification standards and should not be interpreted as governmental approval or licensing.

SEC. 03

Scope and Effect of Certification

Certification:

  • Applies to an entity (breeder, cattery, kennel, or related organization)
  • Reflects status within the ElitePetz certification framework
  • Certification status may change over time in accordance with the Authority's governance policies, including activation, suspension, revocation, expiration, or reinstatement where applicable
  • Is subject to ongoing compliance review
Certification Does Not
  • ✕Guarantee quality, health, or condition of animals
  • ✕Replace veterinary advice
  • ✕Replace government licensing
  • ✕Create a fiduciary or agency relationship
  • ✕Guarantee commercial performance

Certification is a structured status designation within a private framework.

SEC. 04

Public Registry and Verification

ElitePetz maintains a public certification registry. The registry may display:

  • Certification ID
  • Entity Name
  • Country of Operation
  • Species / Breed Category
  • Certification Status
  • Activation Date
  • Expiration Date
  • Status History (where applicable)

Registry results reflect the official certification record available at the time the search is performed.

ElitePetz does not guarantee uninterrupted registry access or real-time availability.

False, Misleading, or Incomplete Information May Result In

SEC. 06

Certification Decisions and Status Records

Certification actions are documented within a permanent administrative record maintained under the Authority's governance framework. Recorded actions may include submission, approval, rejection, suspension, revocation, and reinstatement.

ElitePetz retains sole discretion in certification determinations in accordance with its published policies.

Fees

Certification fees are handled as follows:

  • Applicable certification fees are described during the application process
  • Payment does not guarantee certification approval
  • Where a certification application is rejected before payment is required, no certification fee is charged
  • Any refund policy will be stated where applicable

ElitePetz reserves the right to modify its fee structure.
