Why this registry exists

SEC. 01 · The structural gap the Authority addresses

The companion animal breeding sector lacks a widely available public mechanism for independently verifying certification claims.

01 Prospective buyers frequently encounter claims of certification without a reliable way to confirm what the certification means, who issued it, or whether it remains current.
02 Many certification programs publish little or no public information regarding certified entities, issuance dates, status changes, suspensions, revocations, or expirations.
03 When certification records are not publicly accessible, buyers must rely primarily on marketing materials, screenshots, badges, or self-reported claims.
04 The absence of transparent records makes independent verification difficult and limits public visibility into the lifecycle of a certification.
The ElitePetz Response

The ElitePetz Certification Authority was established to maintain a permanent public registry of certification records, status histories, and verification information. Each certification is assigned a unique identifier, and every status change remains publicly visible as part of the record.

About the ElitePetz Certification Authority

SEC. 02 · Institutional self-description

We are a private certification body operating within the companion animal breeding sector.

Our role is to administer certifications under a published standards framework, maintain certification records, and provide public verification of certification status through the ElitePetz Registry.

We are independent of breeders, breed clubs, kennel clubs, government licensing agencies, and commercial marketplaces. We do not regulate breeding activities, provide veterinary advice, or participate in the sale of animals.

Our purpose is to administer certifications, maintain accurate records, and provide transparent public verification of certification status. Learn more about the Authority →

What ElitePetz certification represents

SEC. 03 · The certification programme

ElitePetz certification indicates that a breeder has been reviewed under the Authority's published standards framework and has been issued a public certification record maintained within the ElitePetz Registry.

Certification establishes a documented status that may be independently verified through a permanent public record.

The certification lifecycle

SEC. 06 · Defined sequence of record states

Every certification record passes through a defined operational sequence — from application to renewal. The status of a record is never ambiguous, and every transition is logged in the public history.

Stage 01 · Application

Application

Breeder submits an application against the published Standards Framework.

Stage 02 · Review

Review

Application reviewed by the certification committee against the framework.

Stage 03 · Decision

Decision

Application approved or declined, with reasoned grounds recorded.

Stage 04 · Activation

Activation

Approved record admitted to the public registry under a permanent identifier.

Stage 05 · Monitoring

Monitoring

Active certification monitored across its term under the published procedure.

Stage 06 · Renewal

Renewal

At term, the record may be renewed under the framework in force at renewal.

Independent Advisory Panel

SEC. 10 · Reserved · Appointments forthcoming

The ElitePetz Independent Advisory Panel provides external counsel to the Certification Authority on matters of standards interpretation, framework revision, and the review of complex or contested cases. The Panel operates independently of the Authority's day-to-day certification function and is composed of veterinary, welfare, breeder, and public-interest representation.

Independence & scope of the Authority

The ElitePetz Certification Authority operates within a deliberately limited institutional scope. Certification is a documented status, not an endorsement, and does not replace the judgement of any other authority that the breeder, the buyer, or the public may consult.

Not A marketplace, breeder directory, or platform for the sale of companion animals.
Not A substitute for veterinary, behavioural, or medical advice in any form.
Not A government licensing body, regulator, or inspectorate.
Not A kennel club, breed association, or pedigree registry.
Not A substitute for independent due diligence by a prospective buyer.
