Understanding the Certification ID

SEC. 01 · Structure of the Certification ID

Every certification record published by the Authority is assigned a unique Certification ID. This identifier serves as the official lookup key for verification and remains associated with the same record throughout its lifecycle.

Certification ID Structure
EP-CERT-2026-0007-K9D
EP
Issuing authority identifier.
CERT
Indicates a certification record.
2026
Original year of certification issuance.
0007
Sequential certification number for that issuance year.
K9D
Integrity suffix used for identifier validation.

The Certification ID serves as the official public identifier for a certification record. It is used to retrieve the corresponding registry record and verify the certification's current status.

What the Registry displays

SEC. 02 · Fields returned on verification

Entering a valid Certification ID returns the public registry record associated with that certification. Verification results display the current status of the certification together with key record information.

Entity Name Name of the breeder or entity associated with the certification.
Country of Operation Primary country of operation.
Species & Breed Category Species and breed category associated with the certification.
Certification Status Current standing of the certification within the registry.
Activation Date Date the certification became active.
Expiration Date Date the current certification term is scheduled to expire.
Most Recent Status Change Most recent publicly recorded status change.
If no matching record is found
No certification record was found for the Certification ID entered. This may indicate an incorrect identifier, a formatting error, or a Certification ID that has not been issued by the Authority.

Purpose of verification

SEC. 03 · Intended use of this lookup

This lookup provides a single authoritative source for confirming the status of an ElitePetz certification.

Audience — 01

Prospective Buyers

Confirm whether a breeder's certification is currently active before relying on certification-related claims.

Audience — 02

Independent Review

Reference source for organizations, researchers, media, and other parties evaluating certification claims.

Audience — 03

Regulatory & Institutional Review

Registry reference for agencies, organizations, and professional bodies reviewing certification records.

Verify directly through the registry
Certification claims should be evaluated using the official registry record. Screenshots, badges, and third-party references may not reflect the certification's current status.

Interpreting certification status

SEC. 04 · Understanding public certification statuses

Every certification record is assigned a public status that reflects its current standing within the registry.

ElitePetz Certified
Certification is currently active. The certification remains in good standing within the registry.
Invited for Certification
Invitation issued and awaiting breeder activation. The record is publicly visible, but certification is not yet active.
Suspended
Certification temporarily suspended. The certification is under review and is not currently in active standing.
Revoked
Certification formally withdrawn. The certification is no longer recognized by the Authority.
Expired
Certification term has expired. The certification is no longer active unless renewed.
Status Changes Are Dated
Where applicable, certification status changes are accompanied by the date on which the change occurred.

What ElitePetz certification represents

SEC. 05 · What certification signifies

An ElitePetz certification indicates that the listed breeder or entity successfully completed the review process under the standards framework in effect at the time of evaluation.

Certification decisions are based on a documented review process designed to evaluate consistency, transparency, and alignment between submitted and publicly available information.

01 Consistency of breeder identity across public-facing channels.
02 Transparency of breeding operations and public representations.
03 Alignment between submitted information and publicly available information.
04 No known material contradictions identified during review.

Certification reflects the Authority's determination at the time of evaluation and should be interpreted together with the certification status displayed in the registry.

Independence & scope of verification

SEC. 06 · What this lookup does not represent

Verification confirms the status of a certification record within the ElitePetz Registry. It should not be interpreted as a substitute for regulatory, legal, veterinary, or other independent evaluations.

Out of Scope

ElitePetz certification does not replace government licensing, veterinary oversight, legal compliance obligations, or breed registry requirements. Likewise, the absence of an ElitePetz certification should not be interpreted as evidence of non-compliance with those requirements.
