Governance · Certification Approval & Status Actions

Decision-Making Authority

Certification decisions are issued by the administrative body responsible for application review and enforcement oversight. Every decision follows a defined process and is recorded against the certification record it applies to.

Types of certification decision

SEC. 01 · Decision Types

ElitePetz issues six defined types of certification decision. Each moves a record through the review process or changes its published status.

Approval

A determination that an application meets the published certification standards.

Rejection

A determination that an application does not meet the published certification standards.

Activation

An approved certification is brought into active, published status in the registry.

Suspension

A certification's active status is temporarily placed on hold.

Revocation

A certification is permanently withdrawn.

Expiration

A certification lapses at the end of its term.

How every decision is issued

SEC. 02 · The Standard

Whatever its type, each decision is issued to the same standard, so the basis for it can always be traced.

Every decision is
  • ✓Based on available documentation and representationsAssessed on the documentation and representations provided, not on informal assurances.
  • ✓Recorded within the systemCaptured in the certification system rather than held outside it.
  • ✓Time-stampedDated at the point it is issued, so the sequence of actions is clear.
  • ✓Linked to the applicable certification recordTied to the specific record it affects and its permanent status history.
The boundary

ElitePetz does not issue informal verbal certifications or off-system approvals.
