About the Authority

About the ElitePetz Certification Authority

A private, independent certification body operating within the companion animal breeding sector. The Authority administers certification under a published standards framework, maintains a public verification registry, and documents the full certification lifecycle as a permanent public record.

Institutional Overview

SEC. 01 · Who We Are

The ElitePetz Certification Authority is a private certification body operating within the companion animal breeding sector.

The Authority was established to introduce structured certification standards, transparent status tracking, and documented enforcement procedures within an industry that has traditionally relied on informal trust mechanisms.

ElitePetz operates independently and is not affiliated with any government agency or trade association. Its function is limited to the evaluation and certification of applicants in accordance with published standards.

Mission

SEC. 02 · Our Mission

The mission of the ElitePetz Certification Authority is to:

Mission Commitments
  • ✓Establish clear and documented certification criteria
  • ✓Maintain a transparent public verification system
  • ✓Record certification lifecycle events in a structured audit log
  • ✓Promote responsible operational practices within the companion animal breeding sector

The Authority does not engage in commercial transactions and does not represent buyers or sellers.

Scope of Authority

SEC. 03 · What We Certify

ElitePetz certification applies to:

  • Companion animal breeders
  • Catteries
  • Kennels
  • Breeding operations operating within defined eligibility criteria

Certification reflects compliance with published standards at the time of approval and during the certification validity period.

ElitePetz does not conduct continuous on-site supervision unless explicitly stated within certification requirements.

Governance Structure

SEC. 04 · How Decisions Are Made

Certification decisions are made through a documented administrative review process.

Each application is evaluated against established criteria. Decisions are recorded within the certification system and linked to a permanent status history record.

Governance Principles
  • Standardized evaluation criteria
  • Documented decision rationale
  • Recorded status changes
  • Structured enforcement procedures
Administrative Actions May Include
Approval Rejection Activation Suspension Revocation

All actions are logged within the system's status history database.

Independence & Conflict of Interest

SEC. 06 · Independence

ElitePetz operates independently from certified entities.

Certification status is not influenced by sales volume, commercial partnerships, or external promotional arrangements.

No certification decision may be altered outside documented administrative procedures.

Enforcement Framework

SEC. 07 · Suspension & Revocation

Certification may be suspended or revoked when:

01Eligibility criteria are no longer met.
02Material misrepresentation is identified.
03Required documentation is not maintained.
04Other grounds specified in the Rules & Standards are triggered.

Enforcement actions are documented and reflected in the public verification system.

ElitePetz reserves the right to review certification status when credible evidence of non-compliance is submitted through official intake procedures.

Limitations of Authority

SEC. 08 · Mandate & Limits

The scope of certification is defined and bounded. The ElitePetz Certification Authority:

Outside Our Authority
  • ✕Is not a government regulator
  • ✕Does not replace USDA or other regulatory licensing requirements
  • ✕Does not mediate private buyer-seller disputes
  • ✕Does not guarantee the future conduct of certified entities
  • ✕Does not assume liability for private transactions

Certification reflects compliance with defined standards within a limited scope and timeframe.

Relationship to Government Licensing & Business Accreditation

SEC. 09 · Independent of Government & Accreditation

ElitePetz Certification operates independently of government licensing programs and private business accreditation bodies.

Government agencies, including the United States Department of Agriculture (USDA), administer regulatory licensing and inspection programs pursuant to applicable federal law. Such licensing reflects compliance with statutory minimum animal welfare and operational requirements.

Private organizations, including the Better Business Bureau (BBB), provide business accreditation and rating services focused primarily on commercial conduct, advertising practices, and consumer complaint resolution.

ElitePetz Certification does not replace, supersede, or duplicate such frameworks. Rather, it evaluates applicants against published ElitePetz breeder-specific standards that address documented health practices, transparency protocols, compliance history tracking, and certification status governance.

ElitePetz Certification is a private, voluntary program. Certification signifies conformity with the Authority's published standards at the time of evaluation and is subject to ongoing review in accordance with established governance procedures.

ElitePetz makes no representation regarding the adequacy or sufficiency of any governmental or third-party accreditation program, nor does certification imply endorsement by any government agency or external organization.
