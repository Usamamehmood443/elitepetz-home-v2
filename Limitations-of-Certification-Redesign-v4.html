Limitations of Certification

ElitePetz certification confirms that a breeder has completed our documented application and review process and met the ElitePetz standards in force at the time of assessment. This page sets out plainly what certification does and does not mean, so breeders, buyers, and the public can rely on it accurately.

What certification does not do

SEC. 01 · The Boundaries

ElitePetz certification is a defined, administrative assessment. It is deliberately narrow, and the following boundaries are as important as the assessment itself.

ElitePetz certification:

✕
Does not guarantee the quality of animals
ElitePetz assesses documented standards and administrative compliance. It does not inspect, grade, or warrant the health, temperament, or condition of any individual animal.
✕
Does not guarantee transaction outcomes
Certification does not guarantee the outcome of any sale, purchase, or placement, including pricing, availability, or a buyer's satisfaction with a transaction.
✕
Does not act as a mediator in buyer-seller disputes
ElitePetz is not a party to transactions between breeders and buyers, and does not mediate, arbitrate, or resolve disputes between them.
✕
Does not replace regulatory licensing requirements
Certification is independent of, and additional to, any licensing, registration, or legal requirements that apply to a breeder. Certified entities remain responsible for meeting all applicable laws.
✕
Does not supervise daily operations
ElitePetz does not monitor, manage, or oversee the day to day operations of certified entities. Certification reflects an assessment at a point in time, not continuous supervision.

What certification does mean

Frequently asked questions

SEC. 04 · Common Questions

Does ElitePetz guarantee the health or quality of a specific animal?

No. Certification assesses documented standards and administrative compliance. It does not inspect or warrant the health, temperament, or condition of any individual animal.

Will ElitePetz help resolve a dispute over a sale?

No. ElitePetz is not a party to transactions and does not mediate disputes between buyers and sellers. You can report a concern about a certified entity's conduct relative to the standards, which is a separate matter from a private transaction.

Does certification replace a license I am required to hold by law?

No. Certification is additional to, not a substitute for, any licensing, registration, or legal requirements that apply to you. Meeting those obligations remains your responsibility.

Does an active certification mean ElitePetz supervises the breeder?

No. Certification reflects an assessment at a point in time, not continuous supervision. A record's current status can always be checked in the public registry.

Every ElitePetz certification is recorded and verifiable.
