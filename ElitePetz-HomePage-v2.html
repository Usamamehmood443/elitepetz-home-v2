About the Authority

SEC. 01

The ElitePetz Certification Authority operates as an independent public registry of certified companion animal breeders. It is not a marketplace, breeder directory, kennel club, or government licensing body, and does not provide veterinary advice. The Authority's function is narrow and explicit: to issue certifications under a published standards framework, to monitor those certifications across a defined lifecycle, and to publish the record so that any interested party can verify a breeder's claim.

Certification is a documented status, not an endorsement. A record may transition through several states across its life — including suspension, revocation, and expiry — and all such transitions are logged in a permanent public history. The Authority does not remove records from the registry after termination. A revoked certification remains publicly visible alongside an active one. Transparency, in the view of the Authority, is structural rather than aspirational.

The Authority is governed by a published standards framework, a code of ethics, a decision-making procedure, and a documented process for the submission and review of concerns. All four documents are publicly accessible from the Governance section of this site. Read more about the Authority →

The certification lifecycle

SEC. 03 · Defined sequence of record states

Every certification record passes through a defined sequence of states. The status of a record is never ambiguous, and every transition is logged in the public history.

Stage I · Application

Submission & review

Application submitted to the Authority. Reviewed by the certification committee against the published standards framework.

Stage II · Provisional

Invitation & claim

Approved record published with provisional status. Breeder receives a claim invitation; record remains public throughout.

Stage III · Active

Certification in force

Active certification status. Record displays in the public registry as ElitePetz Certified for the certification term.

Permitted record states
ElitePetz Certified Active certification in force under the current standards framework.
Invited for Certification Approved record awaiting confirmation by the breeder; publicly visible.
Suspended Temporarily withheld pending review. May be reinstated or escalated to revocation.
Revoked Certification withdrawn. Terminal state. Record remains publicly visible.
Expired Certification term lapsed without renewal. May be renewed to Active.

The Founding Cohort

SEC. 06 · Inaugural certified breeders

The Founding Certified Breeders cohort is a single, capped group of fifteen breeders certified during the Authority's inaugural year. The cohort is recognised publicly but receives no preferential treatment within the lifecycle. Once filled, the cohort is permanently closed.

Independence & scope of the Authority

The ElitePetz Certification Authority operates within a deliberately limited institutional scope. Certification is a documented status, not an endorsement, and does not replace the judgement of any other authority that the breeder, the buyer, or the public may consult.

Not A marketplace, breeder directory, or platform for the sale of companion animals.
Not A substitute for veterinary, behavioural, or medical advice in any form.
Not A government licensing body, regulator, or inspectorate.
Not A kennel club, breed association, or pedigree registry.
Not A substitute for independent due diligence by a prospective buyer.
