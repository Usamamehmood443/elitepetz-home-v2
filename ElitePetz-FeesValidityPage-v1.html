Certification · Fees & Validity

Fees & Validity

The administrative fees associated with certification and the validity period, renewal requirements, and expiration terms that apply once certification is granted.

Certification Fees

SEC. 01 · Administrative Fees

Certification is subject to administrative fees as published at the time of application.

Administrative Fees
Fees
  • ›Cover administrative review and certification issuance
  • ›Are non-refundable once review begins
  • ›Do not guarantee approval

Fees are published at the time of application. Payment of administrative fees does not guarantee certification approval.

Validity & Renewal

SEC. 02 · Validity & Renewal

Certification is valid for a defined period of 12 months from the date of activation, unless suspended or revoked.

Validity at a Glance
Validity Period
12 months from the date of activation
Effective From
The date certification is activated
Renewal Requirement
Submission of updated information and payment of renewal fees
If Not Renewed
Certification moves to Expired status
Renewal

Prior to expiration, certified entities may renew by submitting updated information and paying the applicable renewal fees.

Failure to renew prior to expiration results in Expired status.

Status During and After the Term
Active Suspended Revoked Expired

Certification remains Active throughout the validity period unless it is suspended or revoked. If it is not renewed before expiration, the record moves to Expired status.
