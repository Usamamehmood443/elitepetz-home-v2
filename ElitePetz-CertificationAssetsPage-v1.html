Badge Usage Guidelines

SEC. 04 · Terms of Display
  • Keep the badge linked. Wherever the badge is displayed, it must remain linked to the official verification page for this record so anyone can confirm the current status.
  • Do not alter the artwork. The badge may not be edited, recolored, cropped, or combined with other marks. Use the provided sizes as issued.
  • Display only while active. The badge may be displayed only while this certification is active. If the status changes to suspended, revoked, or expired, use of the badge must stop.
  • Issued for this entity only. These assets are issued exclusively for the certified entity named in this record and may not be transferred or reused by any other party.

Full terms are described in the Badge Usage Policy.
