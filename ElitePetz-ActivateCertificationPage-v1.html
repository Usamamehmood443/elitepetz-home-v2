When You Activate

What activation does

01

Your status is finalized

Your certification status is set to active and your record is published in the ElitePetz Public Registry.

02

Your assets are issued

You receive your official Certified Breeder badges, your public verification link, and your certification assets by email.

03

Your record becomes verifiable

Anyone can confirm your certification at any time through the public registry using your Certification ID.
