SEC. 01

Introduction

This Cookie Notice, issued by ElitePetz Certification Authority ("ElitePetz," "the Authority," "we," "us"), describes the limited use of cookies and similar technologies used to operate the Platform.

Continued use of the Platform is subject to this Cookie Notice and the accompanying Privacy Policy.

SEC. 02

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Cookies help websites function securely, remember preferences, and support reliable operation.

Cookies may be:

  • Session-based (expire when you close your browser), or
  • Persistent (remain on your device for a defined period)
SEC. 03

How We Use Cookies

ElitePetz uses cookies only to support secure operation, authentication, and platform reliability.

We do not use cookies for advertising or behavioral marketing purposes.

Cookies may be used for the following purposes:

AEssential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for:

  • Account login and authentication (Wix Members)
  • Maintaining secure sessions
  • Preventing unauthorized access
  • Ensuring platform functionality

Without these cookies, the Platform cannot operate properly.

BFunctional Cookies

These cookies may:

  • Remember user preferences
  • Remember interface preferences
  • Support structured application workflows
CAnalytics Cookies (Wix Infrastructure)

Through Wix hosting infrastructure, limited analytics cookies may be used to:

  • Understand aggregate platform usage
  • Monitor performance
  • Improve system reliability

Analytics data is aggregated and does not identify individual applicants in the public registry.

Because the Platform is hosted on Wix infrastructure, certain cookies are deployed automatically to support security, hosting performance, and core website functionality.

For additional information regarding Wix cookies, users may consult Wix's privacy and cookie documentation.

SEC. 05

Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow users to:

  • Block cookies
  • Delete stored cookies
  • Set cookie preferences

Browser settings vary by software and device.

Disabling essential cookies may prevent access to login areas or application functionality.
