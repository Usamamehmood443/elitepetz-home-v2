Governance · Certification Compliance & Actions

Enforcement Authority

ElitePetz reserves the right to review and modify certification status when credible evidence suggests that published standards may no longer be met.

Enforcement actions

SEC. 01 · Available Actions

Where a review is warranted, enforcement actions may include any of the following, applied in proportion to the findings.

Action 01

Request for clarification or documentation

A request for further information or records before any change to certification status is considered.

Action 02

Temporary suspension pending review

A certification's active status is paused while a review is carried out.

Action 03

Revocation of certification

A certification is permanently withdrawn.

How enforcement decisions are made and recorded

SEC. 02 · Basis & Effect

Enforcement is not arbitrary. Every action rests on documented findings and is recorded so it can be traced and verified.

Documented basis

Enforcement decisions are based on documented findings and recorded in the status history system.

Effect and disclosure

Certification status changes take effect upon administrative action and are reflected in the public registry.
