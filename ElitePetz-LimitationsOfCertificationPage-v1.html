Certification · Scope & Limits

Limitations of Certification

ElitePetz Certification has a defined and bounded scope. This page sets out what certification does not represent, so that its meaning is understood accurately by applicants, certified entities, and the public.

What Certification Does Not Do

SEC. 01 · Defined Limits

ElitePetz Certification:

Certification Does Not
  • ✕Does not guarantee the quality of animals
  • ✕Does not guarantee transaction outcomes
  • ✕Does not act as a mediator in buyer-seller disputes
  • ✕Does not replace regulatory licensing requirements
  • ✕Does not supervise daily operations

These limits define the boundary of certification and apply throughout the certification period.

What Certification Represents

SEC. 02 · Within Defined Standards
Certification Reflects

Certification reflects documented administrative approval within defined standards.

Certification confirms that an applicant met the Authority's published standards at the time of review. It is an administrative determination, recorded as part of the permanent certification record, and remains subject to ongoing compliance.
