Complaint & Evidence Submission

Concerns regarding a certified entity must be submitted through the structured intake process available under the Contact section. This page explains what a submission must include and how it is handled.

What your submission must include

SEC. 01 · Required Details

To allow a concern to be reviewed properly, every submission must include the following.

✓Item 01

Certification reference ID (if known)

The ID shown on the certificate or registry record, where it is available to you.

✓Item 02

Description of concern

A clear account of the issue relating to the certified entity.

✓Item 03

Supporting documentation or evidence

Any records, correspondence, or materials that support the concern.

Go to the intake process under Contact →

How submissions are handled

SEC. 02 · Intake & Review

Submissions follow a defined intake and review path, not a public posting process.

No anonymous public postings

ElitePetz does not accept anonymous public postings within the registry.

Reviewed internally

All submissions are reviewed internally in accordance with administrative procedures.
