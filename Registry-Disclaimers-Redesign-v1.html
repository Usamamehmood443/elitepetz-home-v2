Registry Disclaimers

What the ElitePetz Registry does and does not represent, and why independent due diligence still matters alongside registry verification.

What the registry represents

SEC. 01 · Limitations of Registry Data

The ElitePetz Registry:

  • ✓Reflects certification status only
  • ✕Does not guarantee future conduct
  • ✕Does not evaluate individual transactions
  • ✕Does not endorse animals, pricing, or services
What a status confirms

Certification status reflects compliance with published standards at the time of issuance and during the defined validity period.

For users of the registry

Users are encouraged to conduct independent due diligence in addition to registry verification. See the verification guidance.

Frequently asked questions

SEC. 02 · Registry Data

Does a registry record guarantee a breeder's future conduct?

No. The registry reflects certification status only and does not guarantee future conduct.

Does the registry evaluate or endorse individual sales?

No. The registry does not evaluate individual transactions and does not endorse animals, pricing, or services.

What does a certification status confirm?

Compliance with published standards at the time of issuance and during the defined validity period.

Should I still do my own checks before engaging a breeder?

Yes. Users are encouraged to conduct independent due diligence in addition to registry verification.
