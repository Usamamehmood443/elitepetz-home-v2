Official Communications · Submission Portal

Contact the Authority

ElitePetz Certification Authority accepts official inquiries, complaints, and supporting evidence through the designated submission channels below. All submissions are reviewed and processed in accordance with the Authority’s published policies and governance framework.

The Authority does not provide consulting services, legal guidance, or advance opinions regarding certification decisions.

Communication Protocol

SEC. 01 · Submission Handling Standards

Every submission received by the Authority follows a documented review process designed to promote consistency, record integrity, and procedural fairness.

All submissions:

✓ Are logged in the Authority’s record system.
✓ Are routed through the appropriate review process.
✓ Are evaluated within the scope of published Standards and Governance documents.
✓ May receive a written response when a response is determined to be appropriate.

Communications sent outside the official submission channels, including direct email, telephone, or social media messaging, are not treated as official filings and are not incorporated into certification records.

Designated Intake Channels

Scope Limitations

SEC. 05 · Matters Outside the Authority’s Function

The Authority’s role is limited to certification, registry administration, record maintenance, and enforcement activities conducted under its published framework.

ElitePetz Certification Authority:

× Does not provide legal advice.
× Does not interpret external regulatory requirements.
× Does not provide operational or breeding consultation.
× Does not participate in commercial transactions.
× Does not offer dispute mediation between private parties.

Submissions outside the Authority’s scope may be closed without further review.

Response Standards

SEC. 06 · Response Discretion of the Authority

Submission of a form does not automatically require a written response. The Authority determines when a response is necessary based on the nature and relevance of the submission.

The Authority reserves the right to decline responses to:

× Incomplete submissions.
× Matters outside the Authority’s scope.
× Requests seeking advisory opinions.
× Duplicate or abusive filings.
Record retention

Every submission — whether or not a response is issued — is retained in the Authority’s record system and may be referenced in subsequent enforcement or certification proceedings involving the same certified entity or subject matter.

Submission does not guarantee a response. Submission does, however, guarantee a record.
