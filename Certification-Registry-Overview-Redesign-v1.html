Purpose of the Registry

SEC. 01 · Why It Exists

The ElitePetz Registry exists to provide public access to certification status information.

The registry allows any party to verify:

  • ✓Whether a certification is currently active
  • ✓The certification reference ID
  • ✓The certification validity period
  • ✓The current status classification

The registry is intended to reduce ambiguity and provide structured transparency. Certification status displayed in the registry reflects the most recent administrative action recorded within the certification system.

Scope of Public Information

SEC. 02 · What Is Shown

The registry displays limited, status-related information only.

It does not publish:

  • ✕Financial information
  • ✕Internal review notes
  • ✕Private communications
  • ✕Buyer or transaction details

The registry reflects certification standing, not operational performance.
