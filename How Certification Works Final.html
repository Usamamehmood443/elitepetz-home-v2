Certification process overview

How Certification Works

ElitePetz certification follows a consistent, documented process—from application and administrative review through decision, activation, the certification term, and renewal or status action. Each stage is recorded to support accountability and reliable public verification.

From application to registry status

The certification process

Certification is not active upon submission or approval alone. Public certification begins only after all activation requirements are completed and the registry record is published.

1

Step 1

Application submission

The applicant submits a certification application through the official ElitePetz application portal.

Required information includes

  • Legal or operating name
  • Contact information
  • Country of operation
  • Species and breed focus
  • Description of breeding activities
  • Agreement to the published standards

Every submission creates an administrative record, regardless of the final outcome.

2

Step 2

Administrative review

ElitePetz reviews the application against its published certification requirements.

The review may include

  • Review of the submitted information
  • Verification of required disclosures
  • Assessment against certification requirements
  • Requests for clarification or supporting documentation

An incomplete response to a request for additional information may delay or prevent a final decision.

3

Step 3

Decision

After completing its review, ElitePetz issues one of two determinations.

ApprovedThe application meets the certification requirements and is eligible to proceed to activation.
Not approvedThe application does not meet the certification requirements at the time of review.

The decision and its date are retained as part of the administrative certification record.

4

Step 4

Activation

An approved applicant completes any remaining administrative requirements, including payment of applicable certification fees. ElitePetz then publishes the certification record in the public registry.

Certification becomes active only after all activation requirements are complete and the public registry record has been published.
5

Step 5

Certification term

Once activated, certification remains valid for the term shown in the public registry unless a subsequent administrative action changes its status.

During the certification term

  • The registry displays the certification’s current status
  • Complaints or evidence submissions may trigger review
  • Material changes may require updated information
  • Status changes become part of the certification history
6

Step 6

Renewal or status action

Before the certification term ends, the certified entity may apply for renewal. Based on the renewal review, compliance findings, or another authorized administrative review, the certification may be renewed, allowed to expire, suspended, or revoked.

Renewed Expired Suspended Revoked

All status actions are handled under applicable ElitePetz governance and enforcement policies and are reflected in the certification record.

Important distinctions

What each milestone means

  • Submission begins the administrative process; it does not indicate approval or certification.
  • Approval confirms eligibility to proceed to activation; it does not by itself create an active public certification.
  • Activation occurs only after remaining requirements are complete and the registry record is published.
  • Registry status is the authoritative public indicator of whether a certification is active, expired, suspended, or revoked.

Documented certification

Trust is built through consistent standards, documented decisions, and transparent records.
