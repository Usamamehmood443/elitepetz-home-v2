Status History Disclosure

How ElitePetz records certification lifecycle events, and what the public registry discloses about a record's status history.

Status Event Tracking

SEC. 01 · What Is Recorded

All certification lifecycle events are recorded in the Authority's internal status history system.

The public registry reflects:

  • ✓Current standing
  • ✓Most recent status action

Full internal administrative notes are not publicly displayed.

Enforcement Visibility

SEC. 02 · Status Actions

When a certification is:

Suspended Revoked

The registry reflects the updated status immediately following administrative action. The registry does not remove records due to suspension or revocation.

Core principle

Transparency of status is a core principle of the Authority.

Certification decisions are made pursuant to the ElitePetz Certification Standards Framework.

Frequently asked questions

SEC. 03 · Status & Disclosure

Does the registry show why a certification was suspended or revoked?

No. The registry reflects current standing and the most recent status action. Full internal administrative notes are not publicly displayed.

Are suspended or revoked records removed from the registry?

No. The registry does not remove records due to suspension or revocation.

How quickly does the registry update after a status action?

The registry reflects the updated status immediately following administrative action.
