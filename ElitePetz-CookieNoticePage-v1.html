SEC. 02

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Cookies help websites function properly, maintain security, and improve user experience.

Cookies may be:

  • Session-based (expire when you close your browser), or
  • Persistent (remain on your device for a defined period)
