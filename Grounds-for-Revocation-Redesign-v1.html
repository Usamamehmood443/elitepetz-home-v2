Grounds for Revocation

Revocation is the permanent withdrawal of an ElitePetz certification. This page sets out the conditions under which a certification may be revoked, and how a revoked record is treated in the public registry.

Status · Revoked (permanent)

When a certification may be revoked

SEC. 01 · The Conditions

Certification may be revoked when any of the following conditions applies.

01

Material misrepresentation is confirmed

A significant false or misleading statement has been established.

02

Eligibility criteria are no longer satisfied

The entity no longer meets the published standards for certification.

03

Required disclosures are knowingly inaccurate

Mandatory disclosures were provided with known inaccuracies.

04

Failure to remedy issues following suspension

Matters raised during a suspension were not resolved.

05

Conduct materially inconsistent with certification standards

Behaviour that substantially conflicts with the certification standards.

What revocation means

SEC. 02 · The Status

Unlike suspension, revocation is a final outcome. The record of it is deliberately preserved.

Revoked status

Revocation permanently withdraws certification status.

Revoked certifications remain visible in the public registry to preserve transparency of record.
