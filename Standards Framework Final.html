Purpose of the Standards

SEC. 01 · Purpose

The ElitePetz Certification Standards Framework defines how applications are evaluated, how eligibility is determined, and which continuing responsibilities apply to certified entities.

Certification is granted following a documented review conducted by the ElitePetz Certification Authority using the standards established in this framework.

Certification remains conditional upon continued compliance. Its status may be modified, suspended, revoked, or allowed to expire when later information affects eligibility or standing.

Eligibility Standards

SEC. 02 · Entry Requirements

Applicants must satisfy the baseline requirements below before a certification application can be considered.

Meeting these requirements establishes eligibility for review but does not guarantee certification approval.

01 Identity and Operational Transparency

Applicants must provide sufficient information to establish the identity of the individual or organization seeking certification.

Applicants shall
  • Provide verifiable identifying information associated with the breeding or animal-related operation
  • Disclose an accurate operating name and valid contact information
  • Identify the jurisdiction in which the operation conducts its activities
  • Maintain a consistent operational identity across public websites, business profiles, and official communications
  • Agree to the publication of certification status and related registry information if certification is granted

Participation in third-party marketplaces that limit public breeder identity does not, by itself, conflict with this requirement, provided the applicant maintains a consistent operational identity in their own disclosures and communications.

02 Operational Disclosure Requirements

Applicants must provide sufficient information describing the nature and scope of their activities to enable a meaningful certification review.

Applicants shall disclose
  • The species and breed categories involved in their operation
  • The nature of breeding, raising, placement, or related animal-management activities
  • The geographic jurisdiction in which those activities occur
  • An accurate description of relevant operational practices

Incomplete, misleading, or intentionally inaccurate disclosures may result in application denial or subsequent certification enforcement.

03 Legal and Regulatory Baseline

Applicants must conduct their activities in accordance with applicable laws and regulations in the jurisdictions where they operate.

Applicants shall
  • Affirm compliance with applicable laws and regulations governing their activities
  • Disclose known regulatory actions, enforcement proceedings, or significant legal matters relevant to the operation

ElitePetz Certification does not replace governmental oversight, licensing, or regulatory authority.

Regulatory findings, licensing changes, or enforcement actions may be considered during review and throughout the certification period.

04 Applicant Declaration of Accuracy

Applicants must attest that the information submitted during the certification process is accurate and complete.

Applicants shall affirm that
  • All information provided to the Authority is truthful and complete to the best of their knowledge
  • Significant changes affecting eligibility or certification status will be disclosed promptly
  • They understand certification may be modified, suspended, revoked, or allowed to expire under ElitePetz governance policies

Knowingly providing false, misleading, or incomplete information constitutes grounds for denial, suspension, or revocation of certification.

What Certification Represents

SEC. 03 · Meaning of Certification

ElitePetz Certification confirms that, at the time it was granted, the applicant satisfied the Authority's certification standards based on the information available during review.

Certification Means
  • ✓The applicant underwent structured certification review
  • ✓Required disclosures were submitted and evaluated
  • ✓No disqualifying information was identified during review
  • ✓The entity agreed to continuing compliance and transparency responsibilities

Certification reflects compliance with ElitePetz standards based on the information available during certification review.

Certification Does Not Mean
  • ✕A guarantee of animal health outcomes
  • ✕A substitute for buyer due diligence
  • ✕Government licensing or regulatory approval
  • ✕A warranty, insurance product, or commercial guarantee

Certification is an independent administrative determination. Buyers, adopters, and other parties remain responsible for conducting their own evaluation before entering into a transaction.

Maintaining Certification

SEC. 04 · Continuing Compliance

Certified entities must continue meeting the responsibilities below throughout the certification period.

01 Maintain Legal Standing

Continue operating in accordance with applicable laws and regulatory requirements governing their activities.

02 Disclose Significant Changes

Promptly disclose changes that may affect certification status, including:

  • Changes in ownership or operational control
  • Significant operational restructuring
  • Regulatory enforcement actions
  • Licensing status changes
  • Other developments that could affect eligibility or certification standing
03 Cooperate With Certification Review

Respond to reasonable requests for information arising from certification monitoring, complaints, administrative review, or other governance procedures.

04 Preserve Accuracy of Public Representation

Ensure that public representations remain consistent with information disclosed during certification review.

Failure to Maintain These Responsibilities May Result In
Suspension Revocation Change in Certification Status Non-Renewal or Expiration

Certification Status Classifications

SEC. 05 · Status Classifications

Every certification record reflects the entity's current standing within the ElitePetz Certification Registry.

Status Meaning Public Visibility
Approved Certification approved internally before public activation Not Public
Invited for Certification Entity invited to activate its certification record Public
ElitePetz Certified Certification active and in good standing Public
Suspended Temporarily inactive pending review Public
Revoked Certification permanently withdrawn Public
Expired Certification term ended Public

Every certification status change is recorded in the Status History log. Together, these records form part of the permanent administrative audit trail maintained by the ElitePetz Certification Authority.

Versioning & Evolution

SEC. 07 · Versioning

The ElitePetz Certification Standards Framework will continue to develop as the certification program expands, operational experience grows, and additional governance policies are adopted. Updates are recorded under Governance → Evolution of Governance Policies.

Unless otherwise stated, certification decisions are governed by the standards in effect when the review is completed.

Ethical conduct obligations applicable to certified entities are governed separately under the ElitePetz Code of Ethics.
