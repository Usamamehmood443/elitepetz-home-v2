The Certification Reference ID

SEC. 01 · Format of the public identifier

Each certification issued through the Registry is assigned a permanent Reference ID. The format is structured so that the year of issuance, the sequence position, and the verification code are all visible within the identifier itself.

Reference ID anatomy
EP-CERT-2026-0007-K9D
EP
Issuing authority prefix — ElitePetz Certification Authority.
CERT
Record type — a primary certification record.
2026
Year of original issuance. Does not change on renewal.
0007
Sequence number within the issuance year.
K9D
Verification suffix — integrity check against fabricated identifiers.

The Reference ID remains attached to the record for the lifetime of the certification, including through any status transitions. A record that is later suspended, revoked, or expired retains its original identifier.

What the Registry displays

SEC. 02 · Fields returned on verification

When a valid Reference ID is submitted, the Registry returns the public record associated with the certification. The record may include the following fields, depending on the current status.

Entity name The legal name of the certified breeder or breeding entity, as recorded at the time of certification.
Country of operation The country in which the breeder primarily operates, as declared on application.
Species & breed category The species and, where applicable, the breed category covered by the certification.
Certification status The current state of the record — certified, invited, suspended, revoked, or expired.
Activation date The date the certification entered the Active state. Blank for records that have not yet been activated.
Expiration date The date the current certification term will expire absent renewal. Blank for records that have not yet been activated.
Most recent status change The most recent transition recorded against the certification, with date and brief description.
If no matching record is found
The Registry will indicate that no active or historical certification record exists for the submitted Reference ID. The absence of a record means that the identifier has never been issued by the Authority — not that a previously issued certification has been removed from the registry. Once issued, no record is ever removed.

Purpose of verification

SEC. 03 · Intended use of this lookup

The verification lookup is provided as an independent reference for parties seeking to confirm the current standing of an ElitePetz certification record.

Audience — 01

Prospective buyers

Independent confirmation of certification status before considering a transaction with a breeder presenting an ElitePetz claim.

Audience — 02

Third-party due diligence

Veterinary practices, welfare organisations, journalists, and other parties conducting verification of certification claims.

Audience — 03

Administrative inquiries

Regulatory, licensing, and administrative bodies referencing a certification record for record-keeping or compliance purposes.

Verify directly through the registry
Certification status should always be confirmed directly through this lookup. Screenshots, badge images, social-media claims, and third-party representations reflect a record at a moment in time; only the Registry itself reflects the record as it currently stands.

Interpreting certification status

SEC. 04 · Meaning of each registry state

A record may carry one of five statuses. Each is shown publicly on the verification result and remains visible regardless of how the certification has progressed.

ElitePetz Certified
Active certification in force under the current Standards Framework. The breeder has been reviewed, the record was admitted to the registry, and the certification has not lapsed, been suspended, or been revoked.
Invited for Certification
Approved record awaiting confirmation by the breeder; publicly visible. The application has been approved by the Authority, but the breeder has not yet completed the claim procedure. The Activation Date is blank until claim is completed.
Suspended
Certification temporarily withheld pending review. A concern has been raised that requires further examination under the published procedure. The certification may be reinstated to Active, or escalated to Revoked. Record remains publicly visible throughout.
Revoked
Certification withdrawn. Terminal state — no further transitions are possible. Revocation is recorded on the public history with a brief reason. The Reference ID and the record both remain permanently visible.
Expired
Certification term has elapsed without renewal. The record may be renewed by application, in which case it returns to the Active state with a new Expiration Date. Until renewed, the record remains visible with Expired status.
Status changes are dated
Each transition between statuses is logged with the date and a brief description of the action. The full sequence of transitions appears in the public history of the individual record.

What ElitePetz certification represents

SEC. 05 · Basis of the certification decision

Certification indicates that the breeder or entity listed in the Registry has been reviewed against a defined set of identity, operational, and public-facing standards at the time of certification.

Each certification record is maintained as part of a continuously managed public verification system and is tied to a permanent Reference ID. The certification decision is reasoned, documented, and made under a published procedure. At the time of certification, the Authority evaluates elements including:

01 Consistency of breeder identity across public-facing platforms and representations.
02 Transparency of breeding activity and the breeder's public representation of that activity.
03 Alignment between publicly presented information and the certification details submitted on application.
04 Absence of known materially conflicting or misleading public information regarding the breeder or entity.

Certification status may change over time due to renewal, expiration, suspension, revocation, administrative review, or other registry actions. The status displayed in the Registry reflects the current standing of the certification record at the moment of viewing.

Independence & scope of verification

SEC. 06 · What this lookup does not represent

The ElitePetz Verification Registry is an independent verification and registry system, maintained by the Certification Authority. It operates within a deliberately limited institutional scope.

Out of scope

The Registry does not replace government licensing, veterinary oversight, legal compliance obligations, or breed registry requirements. A current ElitePetz certification does not displace any of these, and the absence of an ElitePetz record does not indicate non-compliance with them.
