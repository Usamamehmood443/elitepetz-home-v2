Status History & Record Integrity

All certification lifecycle events are recorded within a structured status history log, so the full history behind a certification can be relied on rather than assumed.

What each status history record includes

SEC. 01 · The Record

Every entry in the status history log captures the same set of fields, so each action can be identified and traced.

Status history entry
01
Action takenThe specific administrative action applied to the record.
02
Date of actionWhen the action was recorded.
03
Administrative actor classificationThe category of authority that took the action.
04
Applicable certification referenceThe certification record the action applies to.

Record integrity and the public registry

SEC. 02 · Integrity

The value of the log depends on entries being permanent, and on the registry reflecting what those entries record.

Permanent entries

Status history entries are permanent and maintained to preserve procedural integrity.

Current standing

The public registry reflects current certification standing based on recorded administrative actions.
