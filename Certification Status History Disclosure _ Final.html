Certification Status History Disclosure

This disclosure explains which certification lifecycle events appear in the ElitePetz public registry, which supporting information remains in the Authority's administrative records, and how published records are preserved.

Document typeRegistry disclosure
Applies toPublic certification records
Last reviewedAugust 1, 2026
Record ownerCertification Authority

Purpose and scope

SEC. 01 · SCOPE

The status history is a record of changes to a certification after a public certification record has been issued. It is not a public copy of an application file, investigation file, or internal decision record.

01

Certification record

Identifies the certified entity and shows the certification's current standing.

02

Status history

Preserves dated public entries for applicable changes in certification standing.

03

Administrative record

Retains supporting submissions, review material, reasons, notes, and decision documentation under applicable policies.

Important distinction

An application that is declined or withdrawn before certification does not create a public certification record. Internal application events are not presented as certification status history.

Status changes and record continuity

SEC. 03 · PUBLIC HISTORY

A certification record remains associated with its Certification ID when its standing changes. Suspension, revocation, expiration, reinstatement, or renewal does not create a replacement identity for the same certification record.

ElitePetz CertifiedSuspendedRevokedExpired

Status action recorded

The Authority records the action and effective date in its administrative system.

Public record updated

The registry is updated after the action becomes effective and publication processing is completed.

Earlier entry retained

The new standing is shown without deleting the record's applicable earlier public history.

Authoritative source

The live registry record—not a badge, screenshot, cached page, or third-party statement—is the authoritative source for current certification standing.

Timing, corrections, and limits

SEC. 04 · RECORD INTEGRITY

Publication timing

Status changes are published after the action is effective and the registry update is processed. A short administrative interval may occur between a decision and its appearance in the public record.

The effective date shown in the registry, where provided, governs the dated public entry.

Corrections

Clerical or data-quality errors may be corrected without changing the substance of the underlying certification action. A correction does not remove a valid prior status event.

Questions about a record—or evidence of an apparent error—should be submitted through the Authority's official contact channel.

Scope of disclosure

A public status entry reports standing within the ElitePetz certification system. It is not a government finding, veterinary assessment, legal judgment, or determination about matters outside the Authority's published mandate.

Verify or question a record

SEC. 05 · NEXT STEPS

Use the registry to confirm current standing. Use the official contact channel to report an apparent record error or submit information relevant to a certification.
