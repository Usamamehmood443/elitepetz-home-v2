What to know
After you submit

Public reviews

Frequently asked questions

SEC. 02 · Reporting

How do I report a concern about a certified entity?

Submit it through the official Complaint Intake Form under the Contact section.

What happens after I submit a concern?

Evidence-based submissions may trigger administrative review.

Can I leave a public comment or review on the registry?

No. The registry itself does not accept comments or public reviews.
