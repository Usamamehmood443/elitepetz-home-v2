Founding Certified Breeders

The Founding Certified Breeders are the first entities to complete the ElitePetz Certification process under the Certification Standards Framework.

These entities were among the first certified by the Authority and are permanently recognized as members of the founding cohort.

Founding designation recognizes participation during the Authority's inaugural certification period. It does not create different certification standards, additional privileges, or exemption from enforcement actions.

What “Founding” Status Means

SEC. 01 · Meaning of the Founding Designation

All Founding Certified Breeders are subject to the same standards, compliance obligations, and enforcement framework as all other certified entities.

Founding Certified Breeders:

✓ Were among the first approved under the ElitePetz Certification Framework.
✓ Agreed to early adoption of structured disclosure requirements.
✓ Maintained certification subject to continuing review and status verification.
✓ Received certification during the Authority's inaugural certification cohort.

Founding designation remains part of the historical record. Certification status remains subject to renewal, review, suspension, revocation, expiration, or other status changes under applicable policies.

Certification Status Transparency

SEC. 02 · Public Status Visibility & Record Integrity

Every Founding Certified Breeder is represented by an individual Registry record that documents certification status, key dates, and other public certification information.

Each founding cohort record:

✓ Is maintained within the public ElitePetz Registry.
✓ Has a unique Certification ID in the format EP-CERT-YYYY-NNNN-XXX.
✓ Displays the most recent publicly reported certification status.
✓ Remains subject to status changes in accordance with published Authority policies.

Founding Period Definition

SEC. 04 · Duration of the Founding Designation

Founding designation is available only to entities certified during the Authority's inaugural certification cohort.

Founding Cohort Limit
First 15 Certifications Issued
The Founding designation closes automatically once all founding positions have been assigned or activated.

Once filled, the founding cohort permanently closes. Subsequent applicants receive standard certification under the same Standards Framework but are not designated as founding participants.

Commitment to Integrity

SEC. 05 · Founding Participation & Enforcement

Founding participation does not grant immunity from review or enforcement.

All certifications are:

✓ Subject to periodic review and renewal requirements.
✓ Governed by published certification and enforcement policies.
✓ Eligible for suspension, revocation, expiration, or reactivation where applicable.
✓ Recorded through an auditable status history process.

ElitePetz Certification is built on structured documentation, transparency, and enforceable standards — not marketing claims.

Founder's Statement

On the establishment of the ElitePetz Certification Authority

ElitePetz was created in response to a simple observation: participants in the companion animal breeding sector often have limited access to consistent, independently maintained certification records. The Authority was established to provide a structured framework for verification, documentation, and public transparency.

The Founding Certified Breeders represent the first cohort to participate in this framework. Their early adoption demonstrates a willingness to operate within defined disclosure requirements, compliance obligations, and publicly verifiable status classifications.

ElitePetz Certification is designed to function as a standards-based system, not as a marketing tool. Certification decisions are recorded, reviewable, and subject to enforcement under published governance policies.

The goal is to create a permanent public record system that allows certification decisions, status changes, and historical records to remain accessible, transparent, and independently maintained over time.
