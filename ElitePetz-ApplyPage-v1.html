Apply for Certification

The ElitePetz Certification Authority accepts applications for evaluation under the published Standards Framework. Submission of an application constitutes formal entry into the Authority's record system.

Notice Approval is not guaranteed.

Scope of certification

SEC. 01 · What certification covers and what it does not

Certification applies to entities operating within the companion animal breeding sector and within the defined scope of the Authority.

The Authority evaluates applications solely against the published Standards and Governance documents. Certification status is limited to the Authority's registry.

Certification does not
  • ✕Replace government licensing
  • ✕Constitute legal authorisation
  • ✕Guarantee commercial outcomes
  • ✕Serve as regulatory approval

Review process

SEC. 02 · What happens after submission

Every application passes through a defined sequence of administrative steps before a determination is issued.

Step 01 · Logged

Submission logged

The application is logged into the Authority's record system and assigned a temporary internal identifier.

Step 02 · Reviewed

Completeness review

A completeness review is conducted to confirm that all information necessary for evaluation has been provided.

Step 03 · Evaluated

Substantive evaluation

Substantive evaluation is performed by the certification committee against the published Standards Framework.

Step 04 · Determined

Formal determination

A formal determination is recorded in the Status History system. The applicant is notified of the outcome.

The Authority does not provide advisory opinions or preliminary eligibility assessments prior to formal determination.

Applicant responsibility

SEC. 03 · Obligations carried by the applicant

The applicant bears full responsibility for the integrity of the information submitted and for ongoing compliance with the Standards Framework if certified.

01 Accuracy and completeness of submitted information.
02 Ongoing compliance with the applicable Standards Framework, if certified.
03 Timely notification to the Authority of material changes affecting the certification record.
Warning

Misrepresentation in an application or after certification may result in denial, suspension, or revocation of the certification record.

What happens next
  • 01 Completeness review — the Authority confirms all information required for evaluation has been provided. The applicant is contacted if any item is missing.
  • 02 Substantive evaluation — the certification committee assesses the application against the published Standards Framework.
  • 03 Formal determination — the outcome is recorded in the Status History system and the applicant is notified by email.
  • 04 Public record — on a positive determination, the record is published in the public registry under a permanent Certification ID in the format EP-CERT-YYYY-NNNN-XXX.
Submission does not guarantee approval. The Authority does not provide advisory opinions or preliminary eligibility assessments. The outcome of the review will be communicated only after the formal determination has been recorded.
