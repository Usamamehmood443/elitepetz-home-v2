Apply for Certification

The ElitePetz Certification Authority accepts applications from companion animal breeding entities seeking evaluation under its published Standards Framework. Submission of an application initiates a formal review process and creates an administrative record within the Authority's certification system.

Notice Submission of an application does not guarantee certification.

Scope of certification

SEC. 01 · What certification covers and what it does not

Certification is available to qualifying companion animal breeding entities that fall within the scope of the Authority's certification program and agree to be evaluated against its published standards.

Applications are evaluated using the Authority's published standards, governance policies, and certification procedures. Any certification status granted is recognized only within the ElitePetz Certification Registry.

Certification does not
  • ✕Confer legal authority or regulatory status
  • ✕Represent government endorsement or approval
  • ✕Guarantee commercial outcomes
  • ✕Replace applicable licensing or legal obligations

Review process

SEC. 02 · How applications are evaluated and determined

Each application undergoes a structured review process designed to ensure consistent evaluation and record integrity before a certification determination is made.

Step 01

Application Received

The application is recorded within the Authority's administrative system and assigned a unique application reference number.

Step 02

Completeness Review

The Authority verifies that sufficient information has been provided to conduct a standards-based evaluation.

Step 03

Standards Evaluation

The application is assessed against the published Standards Framework and supporting governance requirements.

Step 04

Determination Issued

A formal determination is recorded and communicated to the applicant. Approved applicants receive certification issuance instructions, including payment information where applicable.

To preserve impartiality and consistency, the Authority does not provide pre-approval opinions, informal eligibility assessments, or advisory determinations before completion of the review process.

Applicant responsibility

SEC. 03 · Responsibilities accepted upon submission

Applicants are responsible for ensuring that all submitted information is accurate, complete, and current, and for maintaining compliance with applicable certification requirements if certification is granted.

01 Accuracy and completeness of all submitted information.
02 Compliance with applicable certification requirements if certification is granted.
03 Prompt notification of material changes that could affect the certification record.
Warning

False, misleading, or materially incomplete information may result in application denial, certification suspension, certification revocation, or other actions permitted under the Authority's governance policies.

Certification Fee
USD $199.00 / year
Charged Only Upon Approval

The certification fee is only payable after a positive certification determination has been issued.

Applicants are not charged at the time of submission. Applications that are denied or rejected are not charged any certification fee.

For approved applicants, the fee covers certification issuance, registry administration, and maintenance of the certification record during the certification term. Certain invitation-based or founding-program certifications may be subject to different fee arrangements.
