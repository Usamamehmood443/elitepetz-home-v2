Governance · Scope and Boundaries

Limitations of Enforcement Authority

ElitePetz enforcement is deliberately narrow. It operates as a private certification body, and the boundaries below are as important as the actions it can take.

What ElitePetz enforcement is not

SEC. 01 · The Boundaries

The ElitePetz Certification Authority is not a public authority. Its enforcement role does not extend to any of the following.

✕

Is not a governmental regulator

ElitePetz is a private certification body, not a public or statutory authority.

✕

Does not impose fines or legal penalties

It cannot levy financial or legal sanctions of any kind.

✕

Does not conduct law enforcement investigations

It does not investigate in the manner of a law enforcement agency.

✕

Does not mediate private disputes

It is not a party to, and does not resolve, disputes between individuals.

What enforcement is limited to

SEC. 02 · The Defined Scope

Within those boundaries, ElitePetz enforcement has a precise and limited purpose.

Defined scope

Enforcement actions are limited to certification status determinations within the Authority's defined scope.

Certification enforcement does not replace regulatory compliance obligations imposed by governmental agencies.
