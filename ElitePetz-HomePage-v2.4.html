Why This Registry Exists

SEC. 01 · The Verification Gap

Companion animal breeding lacks a widely available public mechanism for independently verifying certification claims.

01 Prospective buyers often encounter certification claims without a reliable way to verify their validity or current status.
02 Many certification programs provide limited public information regarding certified entities, suspensions, revocations, or expirations.
03 Without accessible records, buyers must often rely on badges, screenshots, marketing materials, or self-reported claims.
04 Limited transparency makes independent verification difficult and reduces public visibility into certification outcomes.
The ElitePetz Response

ElitePetz maintains a permanent public registry of certification records and status histories. Each certification is assigned a unique identifier, and status changes remain publicly visible as part of the record.

About the ElitePetz Certification Authority

SEC. 02 · What we do

We are a private certification body operating within the companion animal breeding sector.

Our role is to administer certifications under a published standards framework, maintain certification records, and provide public verification of certification status.

We are independent of breeders, breed clubs, kennel clubs, government licensing agencies, and commercial marketplaces. We do not regulate breeding activities, provide veterinary advice, or participate in the sale of animals.

Our purpose is to administer certifications, maintain accurate records, and provide reliable public verification of certification status. Learn more about the Authority →

What ElitePetz Certification Represents

SEC. 03 · Meaning of Certification

ElitePetz certification indicates that a breeder has been reviewed under the Authority's published standards framework and issued a public certification record.

Certification status may be independently verified through the ElitePetz Registry.

Certification Lifecycle

SEC. 06 · From Application to Renewal

Every certification follows a defined lifecycle from application through renewal. Status changes are recorded and reflected in the public registry.

Stage 01

Application

Application submitted for certification review.

Stage 02

Review

Application reviewed and evaluated.

Stage 03

Decision

Certification approved or declined.

Stage 04

Activation

Certification issued and entered into the public registry.

Stage 05

Monitoring

Certification status remains subject to ongoing monitoring.

Stage 06

Renewal
