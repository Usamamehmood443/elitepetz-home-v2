Governance · Structure of Certification Oversight

Governance Framework

ElitePetz Certification Authority operates under a structured administrative governance model designed to ensure consistency, transparency, and documented decision-making across every certification determination.

How certification determinations are made

SEC. 01 · The Basis

Certification determinations are not discretionary. Each decision is made in accordance with four documented foundations, applied consistently to every applicant and certificate holder.

Published eligibility standards

Every determination is measured against the standards published in advance, so applicants know the criteria that apply before they are assessed.

Documented review procedures

Applications are assessed through a defined review process rather than case-by-case judgement, supporting consistent and repeatable outcomes.

Defined status classifications

Outcomes are expressed through a fixed set of certification statuses, so a record's meaning is clear and comparable across the registry.

Recorded administrative actions

Each action taken on a record is captured within the certification system, creating a documented basis for every decision.

How decisions are recorded and bounded

SEC. 02 · Accountability

Consistency depends on a durable record and on clearly defined limits to the authority being exercised.

Permanent record

All decisions are recorded within the certification system and linked to a permanent status history record, so the history behind a certification can be reviewed rather than assumed.

Defined authority

Certification authority is exercised within the limited scope defined in the Rules & Standards. ElitePetz assesses documented compliance and does not extend beyond that defined role.

The governing principle

A structured administrative model ensures consistency, transparency, and documented decision-making.
