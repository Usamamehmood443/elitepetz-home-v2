Why this registry exists Draft copy

SEC. 01 · The structural gap the Authority addresses

The companion animal breeding sector contains a long-standing structural gap: the absence of a public mechanism through which a breeder's certification status can be independently verified by a prospective buyer.

01 Buyers frequently encounter the phrase "certified breeder" without any practical means of confirming what the certification represents or whether it is current.
02 Certifications are commonly issued by bodies that publish no public record of who holds them, when they were issued, or whether they remain in good standing.
03 Status changes — including suspensions and revocations — are rarely disclosed publicly, leaving prospective buyers with no visibility into the ongoing standing of a certified breeder.
04 Where a record does exist, it is typically maintained by the issuing party itself, with no independent means of verification or recourse.

ElitePetz was established to address this gap. Each certification it issues carries a permanent public identifier that can be looked up at any time. The record discloses the current status, the date of every transition, and the reasoning behind any change — including suspensions, revocations, and expiries.

About the Authority

SEC. 02 · Institutional self-description

The ElitePetz Certification Authority is an independent certification body operating a public verification registry of certified companion animal breeders. It is not a marketplace, breeder directory, kennel club, or government licensing body, and does not provide veterinary advice. The Authority's function is narrow and explicit: to issue certifications under a published standards framework, to monitor those certifications across a defined lifecycle, and to publish the record so that any interested party can verify a breeder's claim.

Certification is a documented status, not an endorsement. A record may transition through several states across its life — including suspension, revocation, and expiry — and all such transitions are logged in a permanent public history. The Authority does not remove records from the registry after termination. A revoked certification remains publicly visible alongside an active one. Transparency, in the view of the Authority, is structural rather than aspirational.

The Authority is governed by a published standards framework, a code of ethics, a documented decision-making procedure, and a defined process for the submission and review of public concerns. What ElitePetz certification represents Draft copy

SEC. 03 · The certification programme

ElitePetz certification is a documented record that a breeder has been reviewed against the Authority's published standards framework, has been admitted to the public registry under a permanent identifier, and remains subject to ongoing status monitoring under a transparent disclosure policy.

Certification includes
Standards-based review
Application reviewed against the published Standards Framework by the certification committee. Outcomes are reasoned and documented.
Public certification record
Approved certifications are admitted to the public registry under a permanent identifier of the form EP-CERT-YYYY-NNNN-XXX.
Verification by certification ID
Any interested party may look up a record at any time, without registration, and view the current status and effective dates.
Ongoing status monitoring
Certifications are not "issued and forgotten." Each remains subject to review under a published procedure for the duration of the certification term.
Public status history & transparency
Every transition — issuance, suspension, reinstatement, revocation, expiry, renewal — is logged in a permanent public history, retained even after termination.
Certification does not represent
  • A guarantee of outcome regarding any individual animal placed by a certified breeder.
  • An endorsement of the breeder by the Authority for any purpose beyond the documented certification status.
  • A government licence, regulatory approval, or any form of statutory authorisation.
  • A substitute for veterinary advice, behavioural consultation, or independent buyer due diligence prior to acquisition.

The certification lifecycle

SEC. 06 · Defined sequence of record states

Every certification record passes through a defined sequence of states. The status of a record is never ambiguous, and every transition is logged in the public history.

Stage I · Application

Submission & review

Application submitted to the Authority. Reviewed by the certification committee against the published standards framework.

Stage II · Provisional

Invitation & claim

Approved record published with provisional status. Breeder receives a claim invitation; record remains public throughout.

Stage III · Active

Certification in force

Active certification status. Record displays in the public registry as ElitePetz Certified for the certification term.

Permitted record states
ElitePetz Certified Active certification in force under the current standards framework.
Invited for Certification Approved record awaiting confirmation by the breeder; publicly visible.
Suspended Temporarily withheld pending review. May be reinstated or escalated to revocation.
Revoked Certification withdrawn. Terminal state. Record remains publicly visible.
Expired Certification term lapsed without renewal. May be renewed to Active.

Independent Advisory Panel

SEC. 10 · Reserved · Appointments forthcoming

The ElitePetz Independent Advisory Panel provides external counsel to the Certification Authority on matters of standards interpretation, framework revision, and the review of complex or contested cases. The Panel operates independently of the Authority's day-to-day certification function and is composed of veterinary, welfare, breeder, and public-interest representation.

Independence & scope of the Authority

The ElitePetz Certification Authority operates within a deliberately limited institutional scope. Certification is a documented status, not an endorsement, and does not replace the judgement of any other authority that the breeder, the buyer, or the public may consult.
